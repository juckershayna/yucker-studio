About — aly + shay Studio

Two Heads.
One Voice.

aly + shay Studio is a small, intentional design studio in Zurich. We work at the intersection of brand strategy, design, and visual storytelling — turning good ideas into something lasting.

We believe in care over speed, substance over trend, and brands that genuinely mean something. Every project is handmade, every detail a decision.

What carries us is a mix of conceptual precision and an eye for atmosphere — from the typography on a business card to the lighting concept of a room.

Values

What We Stand For.

01
Care

Better one detail more considered than one less. Design lives through decisions no one sees — and everyone feels.

02
Clarity

Reduction is not a style, but a method. We refine the essential until nothing superfluous remains.

03
Character

We don't design interchangeable identities. Every brand has its own frequency — our task is to make it visible.

04
Commitment

Promised deadlines, honest assessments, transparent processes. Trust is the most important design decision.
